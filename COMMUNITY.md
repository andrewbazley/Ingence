# Community

## Be supportive

Ingence is an ongoing project. Be kind and constructive, and assume good faith. Say what you disagree
with, not what you think of the person.

## Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** post in [Discussions][discussions].
- **Something broken:** open an [issue][issues]. Each form asks for what is needed.
- **A security problem:** report it privately. See the [security policy][security-policy].
- **Anything else:** write to [hello@ingence.org](mailto:hello@ingence.org).

A good question gives the version (`ingence --version`), your system and Python version, the command
you ran, what you expected and what happened. Describe your folders in words.

`ingence teach shapes -p PROJECT` prints the shapes of your file and folder names without the names.
[Reporting a problem without sharing your data][scrub-guide] says what to leave out and how.

Ingence is supported on:

- Python 3.12, 3.13 and 3.14.
- macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon (arm64) and Intel (x86_64).
- Windows 10 and 11.
- Ubuntu Linux LTS releases.

## How Ingence is updated

Andrew Bazley releases updates as they are developed, or as the community finds them.
Ingence does not check for updates, so watch the repository's releases to hear of one.
To suggest a change or share your own, see the [contributing guide][contributing].
